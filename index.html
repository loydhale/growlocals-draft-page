The leaks

You're not short on demand. You're losing the demand you already have.

Most local businesses don't have a lead problem — they have a response problem. Four gaps quietly cost real revenue every week:

Missed calls

Local customers call when they're ready to act. If no one picks up, many won't wait — a missed call can become your competitor's new customer.

Slow replies

The lead came in — from Google, your website, a referral — but nobody responded fast enough, and it went cold by morning.

Follow-up on memory

Follow-up depends on someone remembering. As you get busier, more of it slips — growth shouldn't break your front desk.

Reviews nobody asks for

Happy customers leave without being asked. That's less local proof, a weaker Google presence, and more dependence on paid marketing.
