# GrowLocals.ai Landing Page Shape

## Context
- Audience: local businesses broadly, especially teams that miss calls, respond slowly, rely on a busy owner/front desk, need more reviews, and do not know how to use AI practically.
- Traffic: broad direct/warm/cold traffic to an offer page.
- Conversion: start a free 14-day trial with card required, then complete intake so the account can be spun up and customized.
- Public guardrails: do not mention Assistable, GHL/HighLevel, Extendly, white-labeling, backend vendors, healthcare, Prime IV, or Loyd's other company.
- Brand: GrowLocals.ai clean premium SaaS/local-growth visual language from `context/growlocals-ai-brand-reference/growlocals-ai-brand-reference.html`.

## Offer
**AI Growth Program**
A done-for-you 24/7 AI front desk for local businesses that installs three practical AI systems:
1. Voice AI — answers/captures calls when staff are busy or closed.
2. Conversation AI — chats with website visitors, answers common questions, and captures lead info.
3. Review AI — helps request, respond to, and improve review follow-up.

Commercial terms:
- Free 14-day trial, card required.
- Account spun up immediately after signup.
- Intake form customizes the account.
- Then $297/month, month-to-month.
- $1,500 setup fee waived.
- White-Glove Setup + real human support included.

## Core promise
Stop letting local leads go cold because your team was busy. GrowLocals.ai installs Voice AI, Conversation AI, and Review AI so your business can respond faster, capture more opportunities, and improve reviews without learning AI yourself.

## Page structure
1. Header / offer badge
2. Hero — 24/7 AI front desk, trial terms, proof strip
3. Problem — local demand leaks through missed calls, website questions, slow follow-up, and unrequested reviews
4. Big 3 AI systems — Voice AI, Conversation AI, Review AI
5. Mechanism visual — lead comes in → AI responds → books/routes/captures/review flow → team sees context
6. What happens after signup — card → account spun up → intake → setup → training/launch → support
7. Support differentiator — chat widget, real person, tickets, video call, account fixes
8. Founder/proof — Loyd Hale, founder; 10+ years owning/operating local businesses; $70M+ growth experience; 300+ small businesses using AI software; awards/podcasts where tasteful
9. Pricing/offer box — free 14-day trial/card required, $297/month, setup waived, no long contract
10. FAQ — card/trial, what gets set up, do I need AI knowledge, what if it needs changes, will it replace staff, what happens after trial
11. Final CTA

## CTA flow
Button: Start Your Free 14-Day Trial
Destination: GHL trial checkout/card-required step, then intake form, then onboarding call scheduling.
Temporary page links use `#start` until GHL URLs exist.

## Illustration needs
- Hero: premium dashboard showing calls/chat/reviews flowing into AI response layer.
- Big 3: module cards for Voice AI, Conversation AI, Review AI.
- Setup flow: card/payment → account spun up → intake → customized AI → launch/support.

## Copy tone
Plain operator language. No guru copy. No vague AI magic. Emphasize practical help for local businesses that do not understand AI but want it working.
