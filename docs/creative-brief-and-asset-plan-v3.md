# GrowLocals.ai Landing Page — Creative Brief + Asset Plan v3

Status: planning reset before rebuild. The current live page is not approved and should be treated as a failed draft, not as the design baseline.

## 1. Business Goal

Create a premium conversion landing page for GrowLocals.ai’s AI Growth Program that makes a local business owner feel confident starting a 14-day trial with a card required.

Primary conversion: Start Free 14-Day Trial.
Secondary conversion: Understand how the system works before starting.

## 2. Audience

Local business owners/operators who already receive customer interest through calls, website visits, messages, repeat customers, referrals, and reviews — but lose opportunities because response, routing, follow-up, and review requests are inconsistent.

They are not AI experts. The page should make the product feel practical, safe, and operator-led.

## 3. Offer

AI Growth Program for local businesses.

Terms:
- Free 14-day trial, card required.
- Then $297/month, month-to-month.
- $1,500 setup fee waived for new members.
- White-Glove Setup included.
- VIP Support included.
- Core system should be set up, tested, launched, and optimized by the end of the trial.

Core modules:
- Voice AI.
- Conversation AI.
- Reviews AI.

Public copy guardrails:
- Do not mention healthcare.
- Do not mention GHL, GoHighLevel, Extendly, Assistable, white-labeling, or backend vendor/platform details.
- Do not say “buy more leads.”
- Do not imply AI fully replaces staff.
- Do not imply the AI always books autonomously. Use book / quote / route / handoff language.
- Do not name Loyd’s other company.

## 4. Positioning

GrowLocals.ai installs a done-for-you AI employee / AI front-desk growth helper for local businesses.

Plain-language promise:
“Capture more of the customer interest you already have — calls, messages, website questions, quote requests, reviews, and follow-up — without hiring, training, or managing another front-desk role.”

Strategic angle:
Most local businesses do not need another complicated tool. They need fewer missed customer moments and a better path from interest to action.

## 5. Visual Standard

The page should feel like a premium AI operations product for local businesses — credible, modern, operator-led, and simple enough for non-technical business owners.

Do not make it feel like:
- a template
- a school project
- a cheap agency funnel
- a generic AI dashboard
- a random set of cards
- a dark cinematic AI page
- pasted screenshots

Approved style source:
- Clean GrowLocals.ai light SaaS brand direction.
- White and pale-blue surfaces.
- Deep navy text.
- Primary blue CTAs.
- Green success accents.
- Rounded cards.
- Soft depth/shadows.
- Clear workflow diagrams.
- Polished product-UI illustration system.

Graphic design standard:
- Art direction first, then code.
- One clear visual metaphor: “AI employee operating center.”
- Custom asset system that explains the mechanism.
- Strong hierarchy and whitespace.
- Proof and pricing must feel credible enough for card entry.
- Mobile needs an intentionally designed rhythm, not just stacked desktop sections.

## 6. Page Narrative / Layout

Use the landing-page conversion framework:

1. Header / offer badge
   - Brand identity.
   - Minimal anchored nav.
   - Primary CTA.

2. Hero
   - Headline: outcome + mechanism.
   - Subhead: done-for-you AI employee for calls, chats, quote routing, follow-up, and reviews.
   - CTA: Start Free 14-Day Trial.
   - Trust strip: 300+ small businesses, $1,500 setup waived, 14-day setup/launch path.
   - Main hero visual: GPT Image 2 “AI employee operating center.”

3. Problem / leak
   - Customer interest is already happening.
   - Calls get missed.
   - Website questions go cold.
   - Quote/booking requests need routing.
   - Reviews/follow-up are inconsistent.

4. Mechanism visual
   - Capture → qualify → book/quote/route → follow up/reviews.

5. What gets installed
   - Voice AI.
   - Conversation AI.
   - Reviews AI.
   - Each module gets its own polished product card/visual.

6. Transformation
   - Before: staff bandwidth decides whether interest turns into revenue.
   - After: every inquiry has a managed path.

7. Onboarding / implementation
   - Trial starts.
   - Intake.
   - Setup call.
   - Configure/test.
   - Launch/optimize.
   - Support included.

8. Founder/proof
   - Loyd Hale, founder of GrowLocals.ai.
   - 10+ years owning and operating local businesses.
   - AI software used by 300+ small businesses.
   - ClickFunnels recognition / award image.
   - Proof should be tasteful and confidence-building, not ego-heavy.

9. Offer / pricing
   - Free 14-day trial, card required.
   - $297/mo after trial.
   - $1,500 setup waived.
   - Month-to-month.
   - White-Glove Setup + VIP Support.

10. FAQ / objections
   - Does this only book appointments?
   - Do I need to set it up myself?
   - Does AI replace my staff?
   - What happens after the trial?
   - What support is included?
   - What should be live by day 14?

11. Final CTA
   - Restate the offer and next action.
