# GrowLocals.ai GHL Trial + Intake Spec

## Funnel goal
Start a free 14-day trial with card required, spin up the account, collect business details, and route the customer to onboarding/setup.

## Funnel steps
1. Offer page CTA: Start Your Free 14-Day Trial
2. GHL checkout/payment step
   - Free 14-day trial
   - Card required
   - Converts to $297/month after day 14
   - No upfront setup charge
   - State: $1,500 setup fee waived for new AI Growth Program members
3. Confirmation / intake step
   - Message: Your account is being created. Complete this intake so we can customize Voice AI, Conversation AI, and Review AI for your business.
4. Intake form
5. Onboarding call scheduling or next-step confirmation
6. Welcome email/SMS sequence

## Intake form fields
### Business basics
- Business name
- Website URL
- Main phone number
- Business address / service area
- Business hours
- Primary contact name
- Primary contact email
- Primary contact mobile

### Services and customers
- What services/products do you offer?
- What are your most common customer questions?
- What are your highest-value services or appointments?
- Do customers book appointments, request quotes, call back, or walk in?
- What should the AI never promise or say?

### Voice AI setup
- When should Voice AI answer? missed calls, after-hours, overflow, always, other
- What should it do with new callers? answer questions, capture info, send booking link, notify staff, other
- What caller details should be collected?
- Where should urgent calls be routed?

### Conversation AI setup
- Website chat placement preference
- Common website questions
- Lead capture fields needed
- Booking/quote CTA preference
- Staff notification preference

### Review AI setup
- Google Business Profile link
- When should review requests be sent?
- Who should receive review notifications?
- Any review response tone preferences?

### Access/setup
- Calendar/booking link if available
- CRM/calendar/contact import notes
- SMS/compliance/A2P details as needed
- Logo/brand assets upload
- Anything else the setup team should know?

## Support promise copy
Inside your account, you can use the support widget to chat with a real person, submit a ticket, or schedule video support. The support team can help fix and adjust things inside your account.

## Welcome email outline
Subject: Your GrowLocals.ai trial is active — complete your intake
- Confirm free 14-day trial started
- Explain account is being created
- Link to intake form
- Explain setup team uses intake to customize Voice AI, Conversation AI, and Review AI
- Set expectation for onboarding/training/launch support
